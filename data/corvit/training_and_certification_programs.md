# Corvit Networks - Training & Certification Programs

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/trainings/training-certification-programs/

## Program Areas

**Technical skills:** network design, routing, and switching; cloud computing and
virtualization; programming, DevOps, and automation.

**Soft skills:** leadership and team management; effective communication and
presentation; other professional development areas.

**Certifications prepared for:** Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), Cisco Certified Network
Associate (CCNA), and others across the technical curriculum.

## Program Features

- Expert trainers: industry veterans with hands-on experience.
- Delivery formats: online, in-person, and blended.
- Certification preparation: support to help students pass exams with confidence.

## Target Audience

- IT professionals aiming for career advancement.
- Fresh graduates entering the field.
- Corporate teams upskilling together.
- Entrepreneurs seeking expanded technical skills.

Note: specific enrollment policies and formal exam-support procedures are not detailed
publicly on this page; those would need to be confirmed directly with Corvit.
