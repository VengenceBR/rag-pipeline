# Corvit Networks - Careers

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/careers/

## Work Environment

"When you join Corvit, you become part of like-minded teams that uphold integrity, respect and
responsibility" - the same IRR values that guide the company overall.

## Employee Benefits

Paid leaves, health insurance, provident fund contributions, and EOBI (Employee Old-Age Benefit
Institution) coverage.

## Career Development

Corvit emphasizes helping employees "take big steps, try new things, and create chances to grow"
in their careers.

## Current Opportunities

Actively hiring across multiple roles in Lahore, Pakistan and Dubai, UAE, including:

- Software development positions (Full Stack Developer - MERN)
- Sales and marketing roles
- Financial management (Accounts Executive)
- Customer success positions
- Pre-sales engineering

## What They Look For

Passionate, detail-oriented, strategic, and results-driven professionals across technical and
business functions. Candidates can submit a CV/resume directly.
