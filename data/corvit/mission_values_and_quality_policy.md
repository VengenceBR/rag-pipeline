# Corvit Networks Pvt. Ltd. - Mission, Vision, Values & Quality Policy

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/company/

## Mission

To be the partner of choice for enterprises by helping them imagine, design, develop, and
experience innovative and sustainable digital transformations.

## Vision

We aspire to provide peace of mind for everyone engaging with us.

## Core Values (the IRR framework)

### Integrity
We believe in being ethical, fair and trustworthy in our dealings with each other, with our
customers and the community.

### Respect
We respect the rights, beliefs, ideas and talents of all we deal with, within the organization
and outside it.

### Responsibility
We feel and we are responsible for our decisions, actions, situations & their outcomes in our
professional & personal lives.

## Quality Policy

We at Corvit are dedicated to being the leaders in ICT by achieving customer satisfaction
through teamwork, commitment and continual process improvement.

Corvit operates across 9 countries, and these principles are stated to guide how the company
operates both internally and in its client relationships.
