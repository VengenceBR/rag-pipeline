# Corvit Networks - Solutions & Client Engagement Approach

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/solutions/

## Solutions Portfolio

Corvit provides "a range of innovative solutions to help businesses overcome their
challenges and achieve organisational success," spanning enterprise systems, networking,
security, and communications, including: ERP/CRM, network security, IP telephony, routing
and switching, data centers, servers and storage, surveillance systems, virtualization,
power management, structured cabling, and cloud services.

## How Corvit Engages With Clients

Corvit describes a collaborative, tailored design process rather than a one-size-fits-all
approach:

- "We carefully work with you to design the right network configuration."
- "We focus on your organization and the processes you perform - taking everything into
  account - from existing equipment and applications, to growth planning and future
  system needs."
- "The most efficient business modeling and thus automation is possible only with the
  extensive involvement of the client" - emphasizing client participation as core to
  getting a personalized solution rather than a generic one.

## Security Philosophy

"Security must be a multi-layered approach, and Corvit ensures that all the layers gel
together well to form a strong security posture for your organization."
