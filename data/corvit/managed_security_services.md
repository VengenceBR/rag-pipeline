# Corvit Networks - Managed Security Services (MSSP)

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/mssp/

## Overview

"AI-powered managed security services delivering continuous protection, visibility, and rapid
response for modern enterprises." Corvit provides end-to-end cybersecurity protection through a
comprehensive, integrated security ecosystem powered by advanced technologies, certified
experts, and a 24/7 Cyber Defense Center (CDC).

## The Seven-Pillar Approach

1. **Assess** - identifying vulnerabilities
2. **Protect** - preventive controls
3. **Detect** - real-time monitoring
4. **Respond** - incident management
5. **Recover** - business continuity
6. **Govern** - compliance and policies
7. **Evolve** - threat hunting

## Coverage

Services span network, cloud, endpoint, application, and data security, with continuous
protection from Corvit's Cyber Defense Center and unified security visibility across
infrastructure types.

## Support Model

Organizations receive assistance from certified cybersecurity professionals who design, deploy,
and manage a security strategy tailored to specific organizational needs, combining proactive
defense with rapid incident response and strategic governance frameworks.
