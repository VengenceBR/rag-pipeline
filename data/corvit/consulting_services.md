# Corvit Networks - Consulting Services

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/consulting/

## Approach

"We provide expert guidance and strategic insights to help business achieve their growth
objectives and make informed decisions."

## Service Areas

### IT & Security Audit
Examines IT infrastructure, policies, and operations using industry frameworks like ISO 27001,
COBIT, and NIST.

### IT Services Management
Implements ITSM best practices (ITIL, ISO 20000) to help organizations run a sustainable,
profitable business.

### IT Governance (COBIT)
Assists with knowledge transfer and implementation of efficient IT governance per COBIT 2019 and
ISO/IEC 38500:2015 standards.

### Project Management
Supports clients in managing projects from initiation through closing.

### Business Innovation
Offers certification training and services including brainstorming and the GInI Breakthrough
Innovation Method, Design Thinking, and innovation project structuring. Corvit is a GInI
Authorized Training Provider.
