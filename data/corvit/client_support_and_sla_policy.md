# Corvit Networks Pvt. Ltd. - Client Support & Service Level Policy

Source: https://corvit.com/networks/it-services/

## Support Model

Corvit's Support Services portfolio provides a single point of contact for the support of
multi-vendor networks, with flexibility and choices in maintenance plans tied to Service Level
Agreements (SLAs). Clients get the support services and priority they need depending on the
criticality of their business.

## Response Times

Response times as low as 24x7x1 hrs - meaning round-the-clock availability with the fastest
support tier responding within one hour.

## Maintenance Portfolio

A flexible maintenance portfolio enables clients to achieve maximum value from their network
investments by delivering dependable in-service levels, aiming to lower costs while increasing
productivity.

## Service Offerings

With offerings for technical support, repair and return, sparing and software management,
Corvit's certified professionals provide on-site, on-call and ongoing IT support services.
Clients can reach support through accessible helpdesk channels, including toll-free numbers.

## Complaint Handling & Transparency

Corvit provides a customer support portal that allows clients to track the status and automated
escalation of their complaints through a customized online complaint handling system, intended
to give clients accountability and visibility into how their issues are being resolved.
