# Acme Corp Shipping FAQ

## Delivery Times

- Standard shipping: 5-8 business days, free on orders over $50.
- Expedited shipping: 2-3 business days, flat rate of $12.99.
- Overnight shipping: next business day if ordered before 2pm ET, flat rate of $34.99.

## International Shipping

Acme currently ships to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. International
orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes, which are the responsibility of
the customer and are not included in the checkout price.

## Order Tracking

Once an order ships, a tracking link is emailed to the address on the order within 24 hours.
Tracking numbers follow the format ACM-TRK-XXXXXXXXX and can be looked up at
acme.example.com/track.

## Lost Packages

If tracking shows a package as delivered but the customer has not received it, Acme asks
customers to wait 2 business days (carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early) before
filing a lost package claim with support.
