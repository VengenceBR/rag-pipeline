# Acme Corp Account & Billing

## Subscription Plans

Acme Plus is a $9.99/month membership that provides free expedited shipping on all orders
and early access to sales. Members can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Membership;
cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, with no partial refunds
for unused days.

## Payment Methods

Acme accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with
one other payment method but cannot be used to pay for another gift card.

## Price Adjustments

If an item's price drops within 7 days of purchase, customers can request a one-time price
adjustment refund for the difference by contacting support with their order number. Price
adjustments do not apply during Black Friday or Cyber Monday sales windows.

## Account Deletion

Customers can request full account deletion by emailing privacy@acme.example.com. Order
history is retained for 90 days after deletion for fraud-prevention purposes, then purged.
