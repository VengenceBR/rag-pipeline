# Acme Corp Return Policy

## Standard Returns

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the
item is unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment
method within 5-7 business days of Acme receiving the returned item.

## Final Sale Items

Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout, including clearance electronics and custom-engraved
products, cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 14 days of delivery with
your order number (format ACM-XXXXXX) and photos of the damage. Acme covers return shipping
for damaged/defective items and will send a replacement or full refund at the customer's
choice.

## Restocking Fee

A 15% restocking fee applies to returned items in the "Large Appliances" category (order
codes starting with LA-), unless the item was defective on arrival.
